# Data sources

The code's licences (see README) do not cover the data. Every source keeps its own.

| What | Source | Licence and credit |
|---|---|---|
| Aerial photos (close up) | The government services listed in `FEGlobe/Sources/FEGlobe/Resources/aerial.json`, asked directly by the phone | Each publisher's own terms; credit as named in that file. **Check each licence before releasing**: not all have been verified. |
| App icon | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) II with shaded relief, 1:50m | Public domain |
| Imagery where VersaTiles has none | [Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016](https://s2maps.eu), EOX IT Services GmbH | CC BY 4.0. "Sentinel-2 cloudless - https://s2maps.eu by EOX IT Services GmbH (Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016 & 2017)" |
| Night lights | NASA Black Marble, through NASA GIBS | Free to use; credit NASA GIBS |
| Place names | NASA GIBS reference labels (made from OpenStreetMap data) | Free to use; credit NASA GIBS and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) |
| Terrain | [Terrain Tiles on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) (Mapzen, SRTM, NASA and others) | Open data with [attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md) |
| 3D models | The lists of [mesh.flowsearch.org](https://mesh.flowsearch.org): Esri scene layers and 3D Tiles photo meshes | **Different for every model.** Each belongs to its owner, who is named in the app. Not all of them are openly licensed: check the owner's terms before reusing one. |
| Apple MapKit (optional globe) | Apple | Apple's terms; not open source |
